Our Story

The mwiko never lies

A mwiko is the wooden spoon every Kenyan kitchen is built around — the one that stirs the ugali, scrapes the pot and gets handed down a generation at a time. We named the restaurant after it because that is the standard we cook to: no shortcuts, no powdered anything, just time and attention.

Our stews go on early and cook down slowly. The greens are washed and chopped that morning. The ugali is turned by hand until it squeaks against the pot — the way you know it is ready.

Find us on the ground floor of Azalea Square on General Mathenge Drive, with tables inside and out. Walk in, or call ahead and we’ll keep one for you.
