Our Story

Coast, grill and jiko

We cook from two directions at once. From the Swahili coast come the samosas, bhajias, muhogo fry and Zanzibar mix — fried to order and eaten with your hands. From the grill come the gyros and shawarma, carved straight into a warm pita with pickles, roasted eggplant, hummus and garlic sauce.

In between sits the jiko. It is where the pizzas cook, over charcoal rather than gas, and where the prawns hit the tawa for the chef’s specials — masala, chilli dry, or garlic butter.

On Fridays the biryani pots come out. Find us on the ground floor of Azalea Square on General Mathenge Drive, with tables inside and out. Walk in, or call ahead and we’ll keep one for you.
