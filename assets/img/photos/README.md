# Photos

Drop the real photos in this folder using the exact filenames below. The site
picks them up automatically — there is no HTML to edit. Until a file exists,
the placeholder illustration stays in its place, so the site is never broken
and never shows a missing-image icon.

| Filename | Where it appears | Shape | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Full-bleed background behind the headline | Landscape 16:9 | 1600 × 900 |
| `story.jpg` | Beside "Coast, grill and jiko" | Portrait 4:5 | 800 × 1000 |
| `jiko-pizza.jpg` | Signature plates, card 1 | Landscape 4:3 | 960 × 720 |
| `gyros.jpg` | Signature plates, card 2 | Landscape 4:3 | 960 × 720 |
| `tawa-prawns.jpg` | Signature plates, card 3 | Landscape 4:3 | 960 × 720 |

## Shooting notes

- **Hero** — the room, the jiko or the grill, not a single plate. It sits behind text and
  is darkened, so pick something with depth rather than a busy close-up. Leave
  the left third uncluttered; the headline sits there.
- **Story** — the kitchen at work: the jiko, the tawa, a wrap being rolled. Portrait.
- **Dish cards** — shot from above or at a low 45°, plate filling the frame,
  in daylight near a window. Avoid on-camera flash.
- Landscape orientation for everything except `story.jpg`.

## Before committing

Resize to roughly the dimensions above and save at JPEG quality ~80. Aim for
under 300 KB each — phone photos are often 4–8 MB, which will make the page
slow on mobile data, exactly the customers you want.

If you have a photo of a dish not listed here, add it and say so — the menu
page has no images yet and is the obvious next place for them.
